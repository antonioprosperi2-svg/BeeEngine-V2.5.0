# Code of Conduct

This project follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our pledge

We want BeeEngine to be a friendly place for everyone, including beginners. We do not accept harassment, insults, or personal attacks in issues, pull requests, or any other project space.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind and patient.
- Welcome questions. There are no silly ones.
- Disagree about the code, not about the person.
- Accept that a maintainer may say no, and say it with respect.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Insults, trolling, or sexual language and attention.
- Publishing someone's private information.
- Any other behavior that would make a reasonable person feel unsafe or unwelcome.

## Enforcement

If you see or experience a problem, open an issue on GitHub. The maintainers will look into it and may warn, edit, or block someone who breaks these rules.
